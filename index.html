01 · The problem

Most brands I talk to say one of these.

"We post, but nothing happens."

Content goes out without a hook, a plan or a reason to follow. Reach stays flat and nobody knows why.

→ needs a strategy
"Likes don't pay rent."

Posts look nice but don't lead to DMs, calls, store visits or leads. Nothing connects content to sales.

→ needs conversion thinking
"We'd need 3 people for this."

One person plans, another shoots, a third edits. Briefs get lost and posting slows down.

→ needs one owner

04 · What I'd do for you

Your first 30 days, week by week.

Week 1

Audit & goals

I study your last 30 posts and 3 competitors, then we agree on 3 numbers to move (e.g. reach, DMs, leads).

Week 2

Plan & shoot

A 30-day calendar with hooks and scripts. I shoot or brief the shoot and build the first batch.

Week 3

Publish & test

Content goes live on schedule. I test two hooks per idea and double down on what works.

Week 4

Report & next

A clear one-page report: what moved, what didn't, and the plan for month two.
